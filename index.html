    05 · THE DESKTOP APP

    Screenshots of the desktop app.
    Version 2.0 on a separate test library, September 2026.

    The desktop app is one Python program with one native window and no server. These are the features that most photo libraries do not have.

    Ranking instead of ratingThe app shows a set of photos and you pick the best one. Your picks produce the ranking, and each star level holds half as many photos as the one below it.
    Culling from the keyboard, with UndoP picks, X rejects, R rotates, S stacks a burst. Nothing is stacked unless you ask. Every change can be undone.
    Lights OutPress L and everything except the photo fades. The loupe opens without animation, so there is no wait.
    Combined search and a command listSearch combines text, your albums and labels, and meaning. Typing > at the start of the box lists every command on the screen.
    Days, folders and drivesPhotos are grouped by the day they were taken. Folders from every drive appear in one tree. If a drive is unplugged, its photos stay in the library and show that the drive is away.
    People and labelsFaces are found on your own graphics card, and a person is a name you give to a group of faces. A label learns from you: search cat, mark the one dog as wrong, and the label improves everywhere it is used.
    Works with LightroomRatings, colour labels and keywords are written to sidecar files in Lightroom's format, and Lightroom's edits are read back. Editing is done in Lightroom.
    Measurements, not scoresSharpness for the whole photo, each face and each eye; whether eyes are open or closed; exposure, colour and grain. Each is stored as its own number that you can filter by. They are not combined into one score.

    WHY I BUILT IT

    I wanted my archive on my own drives,
    with good tools to browse and rank it.
    So it takes ideas from both Google Photos and Lightroom.

    FROM GOOGLE PHOTOS

    A timeline to scroll through, and search by meaning: "sunset over water" finds sunsets over water. No filing or tagging needed.

    FROM LIGHTROOM CLASSIC

    A real folder tree, pick and reject, stacks, a keyboard-driven loupe, filters that combine, and a dense layout.

    FROM NEITHER

    Elo ranking. Quick choices between photos show which ones are your best. You tried it above.
